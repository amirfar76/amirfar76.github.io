About

I am a Research Associate at the Institute for Intelligent Networked Systems (INSI), Northeastern University London, supported by the European Research Council. My research sits at the intersection of information theory, statistical inference, and machine learning, with a focus on building trustworthy AI systems.

My recent work develops principled methods for conformal prediction, hypothesis testing, and information-theoretic risk control — ensuring that machine learning models provide reliable, calibrated guarantees rather than just point predictions. I am particularly interested in hyperparameter optimization, anomaly detection, graph-based learning, and wireless communication systems.

Previously, I was a Research Associate at Oriel College, University of Oxford, where I also tutored undergraduate engineering courses.
