Teaching

I have served as a tutor for undergraduate engineering students at Oriel College, University of Oxford. Tutoring at Oxford involves small-group and one-on-one sessions that complement the departmental lecture programme, with a focus on developing deep conceptual understanding and problem-solving skills.

Oriel College, University of Oxford
  • A2
    A2 Information Engineering Systems
    Second-year undergraduate course covering signals and systems, communication theory, and the mathematical foundations of information engineering.
  • P1
    P1 Mathematics
    First-year undergraduate mathematics for engineering students, covering linear algebra, calculus, differential equations, and probability theory.
  • B2
    B2 Engineering in Society
    Explores the broader societal, ethical, and professional dimensions of engineering practice, including sustainability, safety, and responsibility.
