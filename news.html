New Monograph on Statistically Valid Hyperparameter Selection
The preprint of "Statistically Valid Hyperparameter Selection: From Tuning to Guarantees" is now available on arXiv. The monograph addresses how hyperparameter selection plays a central role in the development of modern machine learning, and presents a unified framework for obtaining statistical guarantees on the selected hyperparameters.

NeurIPS 2025 Tutorial: From Tuning to Guarantees
Delivered a tutorial presentation titled "From Tuning to Guarantees: Statistically Valid Hyperparameter Selection" at NeurIPS 2025. The tutorial covered the theoretical foundations of conformal and hypothesis-testing-based approaches to hyperparameter optimization with formal statistical guarantees.
